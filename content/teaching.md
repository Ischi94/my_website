## Understanding environmental change through evidence

My teaching at the University of Passau spans physical geography, climatology and biodiversity. I aim to connect environmental questions with quantitative reasoning: how observations are made, which assumptions underpin an analysis, and what the evidence can support.

## Teaching at Passau

Courses listed on my university profile for **winter semester 2026/27** include:

- Biogeography and Global Change
- Klimatologie
- Einführung Physische Geographie
- Einführung Geographische Regionalforschung
- Mensch–Umwelt-Fragen
- Abschlussarbeitskolloquium Physische Geographie

In **summer semester 2026**, my teaching also included Biodiversity and Species Extinction, Angewandte Physische Geographie and the physical geography thesis colloquium.

## Learning quantitative methods

My open tutorials cover R, data visualisation, statistical tests and Bayesian reasoning. They provide worked examples that make analytical decisions visible. These materials are an archive of teaching and learning resources; package versions and examples reflect their original publication dates.

[Browse R and statistics resources](/resources/)

## Thesis discussions and mentoring

I welcome enquiries about research questions at the intersection of biodiversity, environmental change and quantitative methods. Students can contact me to discuss topic fit and the relevant programme requirements; supervision arrangements and availability are agreed individually.
