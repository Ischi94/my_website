## Connecting Earth's history with biodiversity today

I am a researcher and lecturer in Physical Geography at the University of Passau, where I work at the Chair of Physical Geography with a focus on Human–Environment Research. My research connects palaeobiology, macroecology and applied statistics to understand biodiversity responses to environmental change.

I completed my PhD at the University of Bayreuth in 2023, graduating *summa cum laude*. My doctoral research examined the role of climate legacies in shaping extinction risk. Subsequent research at the University of Zurich, Friedrich-Alexander University Erlangen–Nürnberg and Johannes Kepler University Linz extended this work to extinction dynamics, marine biodiversity conservation and bias in the fossil record. I joined Passau in June 2025.

## Collaboration and open science

I welcome conversations about biodiversity change, fossil and ecological data, Bayesian modelling and causal inference. My work connects researchers in Earth science, ecology, geography and statistics. At Linz, I held an independently acquired interdisciplinary postdoctoral position focused on identifying and correcting biases in the fossil record.

Open software, reproducible analyses and accessible research materials are central to how I work. [Explore my code on GitHub](https://github.com/Ischi94) and [browse teaching resources](/resources/).
